# Cards stand

A parametric cards stand constructor with a named 2D sketch and an OpenSCAD-generated Three.js preview.

Use Node.js 24, then run `npm install` and `npm run dev`. Run `npm run build` for production and `npm test` to verify the actual OpenSCAD mesh geometry.

The sidebar and viewport styles follow the sibling dome-builder project. Sketch has seven tabs: Cell top (one cell in XOY), Cell side (the base end in YOZ), Hinge top (XOY: outer 1–4 from hinge top width, hinge height, cell width, and the hinge centre 1–2 distance), Hinge side (YOZ: a circle at the origin with diameter `wall thickness`), Bolt (XOY: a red circle of radius `(base height - hinge tolerance) / 2` and a green circle of the Cell side stand hinge circle's radius minus `bolt tolerance`), Stand top (XOY: outer 1 and outer 2 at X = `(wall thickness * (columns + 1) + cell width * columns) / 2` and Y = Y top 1 / -stand length, with outer 3 and outer 4 their projections on OY, joined as the outline outer 1 → outer 2 → outer 3 → inner 4 → inner 3 → inner 2 → inner 1 → outer 4, rounded at inner 3 and inner 2 by `stand profile width / 2`; inner 1 is `(0, -stand profile width)` and inner 2 is `(outer 1 X - stand profile width, -stand profile width)`; inner 3 and inner 4 share inner 2 and inner 1's X at `outer 3 Y + stand profile width`; see below), and Stand side (YOZ, positive Y left: a green outline: the top half-circle arc of diameter `base height - stand hinge tolerance` from outer 1 to outer 2 on the Y axis, then outer 2 → outer 3 → outer 4 → outer 1, around the Cell side red circle; outer 3 and outer 4 are outer 2 and outer 1 moved down by the Cell side yellow arc's radius, `base height / 2 + stand tolerance`; outer 5 and outer 6 are outer 3 and outer 4 moved down by `wall thickness`, with a purple polygon outer 3 → outer 5 → outer 6 → outer 4). Sketches show named points, hover formulas, and dash-dot axes. 3D shows the repeated cell layout. Drag to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Fit model frames the generated mesh; Redraw rebuilds it. The selected view (Sketch/3D/Assembly), sketch tab, 3D model, and sidebar parameters are remembered across reloads in localStorage; Reset to defaults restores every parameter.

The first cell keeps its sketch coordinates. Columns advance in +X by `cell width + wall thickness`; rows advance in -Y by `cell length + 2 * wall thickness + (hinge thickness + hinge tolerance) / sin(base angle)`.

Each cell unions three extrusions. The floor is the outer rectangle (cell outer 1–4) extruded by wall thickness. Full raised profiles (cell outer mid 1 → cell outer 1 → cell outer 2 → rim R2 → rim R1 → cell inner 2 → cell inner 1 → cell inner mid 1, and its Y-axis mirror) extrude by the full base height. Semi raised profiles (cell inner mid 2 → rim R1 → rim R2 → cell outer mid 2, and its mirror) extrude by `base height - hinge thickness - hinge tolerance`. All start at Z=0, with extrusion distances measured along Z. Extrusions rise toward +Y and +Z, at the base angle measured from +Y in the YOZ plane. The Y shift is `Z / tan(base angle)`; that shear is computed in TypeScript and passed to OpenSCAD so cell faces coincide exactly with the base end. Angles must be greater than 0° and at most 90°; base height must exceed hinge thickness plus tolerance. Rim R1 and R2 sit at `cell width / 2 - rim width`, at `-cell length` and `-cell length - wall thickness`.

`src/sketch.ts` defines the points, including outer/inner midpoint markers. `src/model.ts` passes the same inner rectangle coordinates to OpenSCAD along with layout and extrusion parameters. `src/models/base.scad` defines the angled extrusion and repeated cell union. The official OpenSCAD WebAssembly runtime is installed in `public/vendor/openscad` with source, version, checksum, and license details.

The 3D tab switches between models: Base, Bolt (the Bolt sketch's red circle extruded by wall thickness, and its green circle extruded by `bolt length + wall thickness` with a 45° end chamfer of `green radius / 3`), and Hinge (the Hinge top blue outline, with sampled fillets, extruded symmetrically about Z = 0 by wall thickness in total, unioned with the Hinge side circle extruded along X from inner 3 by `hinge profile width + wall thickness`, all mirrored across the ZOY plane and repeated per column at the cell column pitch; neighbouring hinges join through their overlapping cylinders and the green bridge polygon, extruded like the blue outline between neighbours only, never at the model's outer sides). OpenSCAD runs in an isolated background worker and exports a binary STL in millimeters. Three.js loads that mesh, converts Z-up to Y-up, and fits the actual mesh bounds. Superseded builds are terminated; the last successful mesh remains visible during rebuilding or invalid edits. Export/download UI is a future step.

In the cell sketch and row layout, the hinge span along Y is `(hinge thickness + hinge tolerance) / sin(base angle)`, with top points at `wall thickness + (hinge thickness + hinge tolerance) / sin(base angle)`. X coordinates retain the original hinge tolerance; extrusion distances along Z are unchanged.

Outer 4 sits on the base end circle with its radius increased by `stand tolerance` (same centre); outer 3 is where the line from outer 4 at the stand angle meets Z = base height, and outer 5 is where the enlarged circle crosses Z = 0 on the +Y side of the centre. The stand angle must keep outer 4 between Z = 0 and base height.

The base end polygon (rest 1 → outer 2 → outer 1 → rest 2) is extruded along X across the full model width and fused to row 0 along its outer 1–outer 2 face. The yellow outline (rest 2 → outer 5 → arc → outer 4 → outer 3 → rest 1, closed back to rest 2) is extruded the same way but inset by `2 * wall thickness` from each side. The pink circle (diameter `base height - 2 * wall thickness - stand hinge tolerance`, concentric with the helper circle) is drilled through the yellow extrusion along X, with 45° chamfers of `wall thickness / 2` at both ends. Neither extrusion is subtracted by the hinge cuts.

The purple outline from the Base end sketch (outer 2 → mid cut 1 → mid cut 2 → arc → mid cut 3 → hinge circle 2 bottom → arc → its tangent point on outer 1–outer 2) is shifted toward +Y by `cutOutlineEpsilon` (0.001 mm) and cut along X through every row, between the inner faces of the two outer side walls, so only those walls remain in that region.

The hinge capsule from the Base end sketch is subtracted along X at both sides of every cell, in every row. Each cut starts at the outer face of the side wall and runs inward by `wall thickness * 2 + hinge profile width + hinge tolerance`. The capsule sketch and cuts share their geometry through `src/base-end-geometry.ts`.

Stand top values: `Y top 1` is the Cell side outer 3–outer 4 length; `Y top 2` is the Cell side distance from outer 1 to the circle centre's projection on OY; `full length = Y top 2 + (cell outer 1–outer 2 length) * rows`; `stand length = full length * sin(stand angle) / sin(stand angle + base tilt angle)`. Stand angle + base tilt angle must be less than 180°.

Stand model: the Stand top blue outline extruded along -Z by wall thickness from Z = Stand side outer 3 Z, unioned with the Stand side green outline (half circle plus outer 2 → outer 3 → outer 4), minus its red circle, extruded along X by wall thickness from X = Stand top outer 1 X toward O; everything is mirrored across the YOZ plane.

Assembly mode (next to Sketch and 3D) places the parts together, each part type in its own colour (base blue, hinge green, bolt yellow, stand purple). It builds only while visible and rebuilds after parameter changes. Its sidebar section has Stand extended and Hinge extended options, remembered across reloads; the assembly holds the base, hinge, and stand, placed by the rules in `src/assembly.ts` (rule 1: each part is centred on X = 0; rule 2: one hinge per row; with Hinge extended its axis is on Cell side hinge centre 1 with the plate running up along outer 1 → outer 2, otherwise on hinge centre 2 with the plate running toward mid cut 1); Stand extended has no effect yet.
